# Code of Conduct

diskdash is a small project that welcomes people of every background and level of experience. We want it to be a place where questions are safe to ask and disagreements stay about the work.

## Our standard

- Be kind and patient. Assume good faith.
- Be respectful of different opinions, experience levels and ways of working.
- Give feedback on the code and the ideas, not the person. Receive feedback gracefully.
- No harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, insults, or sexual or violent language and imagery.
- No publishing other people's private information (including their disk contents, paths or serial numbers) without permission.
- Keep reports and reviews constructive and on topic.

## Scope

This applies in all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide or remove comments, close threads, and block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's private **Report content** option on the comment, or contact the maintainers through the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form and say it is a conduct report. Reports are handled privately and in good faith.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face consequences decided by the other maintainers.
